## Brand Personality
Professional, trustworthy, modern, calm, academic, global, and human.

## Visual Principles
Clarity before decoration, content first, generous whitespace, strong typography, consistent rhythm, authentic educational photography, subtle motion.

## Typography
Inter with system sans-serif fallback.

## Color Direction
White and warm gray backgrounds, near-black text, restrained blue and gold accents. Centralize final tokens in code.

## Final Principle
Great educational design removes friction and creates confidence.
