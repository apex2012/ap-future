## Purpose
Define AP Future's mission, vision, positioning, audience, and long-term direction.

## Mission
Help students build academic knowledge, learning skills, meaningful experiences, and confidence for university success and lifelong learning.

## Vision
Build a trusted international education platform combining educational expertise, thoughtful product design, and responsible AI.

## Positioning
AP Future is not a traditional tutoring site or sales-driven admissions agency. It integrates AP, SAT, University Planning, Resources, and AI-assisted learning.

## Audience
Primary: Grade 8–12 students and parents planning international university admissions. Secondary: teachers, counselors, and education partners.

## Version 1
Public website, program hubs, detail pages, Resources, About, Consultation, responsive design, SEO, and GEO foundations.

## Final Principle
Prepare students for university—and for the future beyond university.
