# AP Future

# 21 · Brand Voice & Writing System

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official  
**Owner:** AP Future  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03

## Voice
Professional, clear, helpful, educational, encouraging, confident, honest, and global.

## Avoid
Sales pressure, exaggeration, jargon, clickbait, fear-based language, and cold corporate writing.

## Hero Copy
Headline 3–10 words, supporting text 20–40 words, one primary CTA, outcome-focused.

## Preferred CTAs
Book a Consultation, Explore AP Courses, Explore SAT, Explore Resources, Start Planning, Download the Guide.

## Terminology
AP Courses, Digital SAT, University Planning, Research Projects, Portfolio Development, Resources, Book a Consultation.

## Final Principle
Every sentence should help readers make better educational decisions.
