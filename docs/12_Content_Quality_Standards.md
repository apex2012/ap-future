# AP Future

# 12 · Content Quality Standards

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official  
**Owner:** AP Future  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03

## Required Quality
Educationally valuable, accurate, clear, practical, original, consistent, accessible, search-ready, and human-reviewed.

## Prohibited
Fabricated facts, statistics, quotes, rankings, citations, testimonials, outcomes, keyword-stuffed filler, copied content, unsupported guarantees, and unreviewed AI drafts.

## Final Principle
Quality always comes before quantity.
