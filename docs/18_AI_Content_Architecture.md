# 18 · AI Content Architecture

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official  
**Owner:** AP Future  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03

## Philosophy
AI accelerates. Educators verify and approve.

## Workflow
Topic → Research → Outline → AI Draft → Human Review → Fact Check → SEO/GEO → Publish → Update.

## AI May
Brainstorm, outline, summarize, improve grammar, suggest metadata, translate, and draft FAQs.

## AI May Not
Fabricate facts/citations, invent rankings/statistics, publish without review, or replace educational judgment.

## Final Principle
AI should amplify educational expertise, not replace it.
