# AP Future

# 01 · Product Requirements Document

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official  
**Owner:** AP Future  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03

## Objectives
1. Build trust.
2. Explain AP, SAT, University Planning, and Resources.
3. Generate qualified consultation requests.
4. Establish scalable SEO and GEO.
5. Create a maintainable foundation.

## Functional Requirements
Responsive navigation, hub and detail pages, reusable components, consultation form, internal links, metadata, sitemap, structured data, and semantic HTML.

## Non-Functional Requirements
Fast, mobile-first, accessible, maintainable, search-friendly, and scalable.

## Out of Scope
CMS, database, authentication, payments, portals, LMS, and AI tutor.

## Acceptance Criteria
All approved pages exist, navigation matches the sitemap, components are reused, accessibility and SEO are implemented, and the consultation flow works.
