# 03B · Content Design Specification

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official  
**Owner:** AP Future  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03

## Standard Structure
Article information, Hero, Learning Objectives, Introduction, Main Content, Examples, Key Takeaways, Related Resources, FAQ, CTA.

## Principles
Education first, one problem per content item, practical value, evidence-based, evergreen where possible, human review required.

## SEO & GEO
Unique metadata, direct answers, clear headings, structured lists, FAQs, and internal links.

## Final Principle
Content is a long-term educational product, not marketing filler.
